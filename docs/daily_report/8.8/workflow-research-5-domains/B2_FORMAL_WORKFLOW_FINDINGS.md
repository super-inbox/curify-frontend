# B2 Formal Workflow Findings

Five evidence-backed formal external workflows, one per domain, built from B1 candidate
research and independently re-verified (via direct WebFetch of live source pages) during

## MERCH — merch_001

**Selected source:** "把自己的插画做成产品：从构思到销售变现的心路历程" (ZCOOL, independent
illustrator 安娜/Anna) — a first-person account of turning campus-architecture
illustrations into postcards/phone cases/magnets/mugs (NUS/NTU, Singapore).

**Why selected:** Highest-scored B1 candidate (MERCH-01); the only candidate with a
genuine, explicitly numbered 12-section end-to-end production trail (reference gathering
through post-sale financial reporting), as opposed to MERCH-02 (business narrative with
no step sequence) or MERCH-04 (Behance final-output gallery only).

**Verified workflow sequence (12 steps):** reference gathering & conceptualization ->
illustration drawing pipeline -> per-format product design/recomposition -> packaging
design -> manufacturer sampling/vendor selection -> legal/trademark clearance -> pricing
strategy -> incoming QC -> listing/product photography -> marketing/promotion prep ->
logistics & delivery packaging -> ongoing operations.

**Professional knowledge:** trademark/IP exclusion rules for fan-made institutional
merchandise; factory MOQ economics forcing manual-branding workarounds at small batch
sizes; per-format illustration recomposition requirements; QC defect taxonomies;
channel-driven protective-packaging needs.

**Evidence strength:** 12/12 steps DIRECT-evidence-backed, 1:1 step-to-evidence mapping,
independently re-verified.

**Important limitations:** single first-person source; self-reported figures (defect
rates, losses, MOQ numbers) not independently corroborated; no embedded images retrieved.

**Ready for B3:** YES.

## ECOMMERCE — ecommerce_001

**Selected source:** "从原片到高转化，天猫详情页制作的设计新思路" (Meitu Design Studio /
designkit.cn) — a vendor tutorial walking through AI-assisted Tmall detail-page
production from a single raw phone photo.

**Why selected:** Highest re-verified B1 candidate (ECOM-01); its source page explicitly
names and orders 5 production sub-steps under one heading, unlike ECOM-02 (10-brand
metrics roundup, no single process trail), ECOM-03 (re-fetched and confirmed narrative/
outcome-focused, no labeled steps), or ECOM-04 (cross-platform strategic comparison).

**Verified workflow sequence (5 steps):** raw photo intake -> core selling-point
instruction input -> style selection -> multi-category image-suite generation -> final
generation & page placement.

**Professional knowledge:** platform image-count/composition/aspect-ratio requirements
(min 7 images, 4 categories, mandatory white-background shot, >=60% product-body
composition, 3:4 vertical ratio); benefit-language translation of raw specs;
CTR-informed style selection.

**Evidence strength:** 5/5 steps DIRECT-evidence-backed; two supplementary claims
(problem framing, efficiency claim) correctly kept PARTIAL and out of the step-evidence
chain.

**Important limitations:** vendor content promoting Meitu's own tool; efficiency/CTR
claims self-reported with no disclosed methodology; single worked example.

**Ready for B3:** YES.

## PACKAGING — packaging_001

**Selected source:** "Getting a Unique New-to-Market Pet Product Into Shelf-Ready
Packaging for Major Retailers" (Catalpha Advertising & Design agency blog) — a real
client engagement for a pet-grooming product ("Combat Comb").

**Why selected:** Tied top B1 score with PACK-01 (both 9/10); selected over PACK-01 per
the tie-break rule (clearer causal step-to-step handoff chain, native-English verbatim
quotes reducing translation-interpretation risk) after both were independently
re-verified. PACK-03 (Pearlfisher/McDonald's) and PACK-04 (generic industry advice) were
not live contenders.

**Verified workflow sequence (6 steps):** measuring up (size/type/structure) -> mockup
testing (fit/structural integrity) -> initial creative exploration (4 concepts vs. 3
named goals) -> dieline drafting (with periodic 3D verification) -> final package
production (layout on dieline, re-verified in 3D) -> product launch.

**Professional knowledge:** structural package-type selection driven by shelf-space
optimization; physical-mockup gating before digital dieline work; dieline must jointly
satisfy folding/stock-weight/print-feasibility; 3D-vs-flat verification at two distinct
checkpoints; named client-approval gates.

**Evidence strength:** 6/6 steps DIRECT-evidence-backed with verbatim quotes; image
captions used only as corroboration, never as sole ordering evidence.

**Important limitations:** single first-party agency source; no disclosed
budget/timeline/retailer names/numeric material specs; feedback-round count undisclosed.

**Ready for B3:** YES.

## BRAND_LOGO — brand_logo_001

**Selected source:** "CASE STUDY: Constellation Rebrand and Launch Campaign" (Behance,
Sabrina Young RGD) — a real, named rebrand project for Canada Life's Constellation
Managed Portfolios program, with a named client-side testimonial.

**Why selected:** Traded lower institutional source authority (individual practitioner
portfolio vs. official consultancy publications) for stronger process visibility and a
concrete, documented professional constraint (multilingual localization + third-party
vendor coordination) tied to a real, dated project.

**Verified workflow sequence (8 steps):** define rebrand objectives -> develop client
personas -> develop advisor personas -> create distinguishing visual device (keyline
square) -> localize personas/materials for Mandarin/Cantonese -> produce multi-format
applications -> coordinate digital-tool updates with external vendor -> launch campaign.

**Professional knowledge:** sub-brand distinguishing-device pattern within a parent
identity system; localization requires adapting personas themselves, not just
translating copy; explicit vendor-coordination step required when a deliverable surface
is externally maintained.

**Evidence strength:** 8/8 steps DIRECT-evidence-backed, independently re-verified across
5 total WebFetch passes (4 original + 1 during this recovery).

**Important limitations:** the single largest caveat of the four "real production"
domains — the source is topic-organized, not chronologically labeled, so step **order**
is a text-dependency-based reconstruction (MEDIUM confidence), though step existence/
content is HIGH confidence and DIRECT-evidence-backed; no sketches/iteration imagery;
self-reported financial/adoption figures not independently corroborated.

**Ready for B3:** YES, with the order-confidence caveat carried forward.

## EDUCATION — education_001

**Selected source:** "深度拆解：月营收3亿的"斑马ai课"长啥样？" (人人都是产品经理/woshipm.com)
— third-party product-analysis journalism on 斑马英语/斑马AI课 (Zebra AI Class), a real,
high-revenue commercial product.

**Why selected:** Highest-scored B1 candidate (EDU-01, 9/10); near-exact structural match
to Curify's own recorded education baseline ("story/episode -> word cards -> reading &
translation -> quiz -> character map -> full learning pack"), with real pedagogical-
science backing. Selected over EDU-02 (Duolingo Learning Designer case — real
instructional-design artifact but a single-pattern internship exercise, not a shipped
product) and EDU-03 (Twinkl official process page — high authority but entirely generic,
no single example project).

**Verified content sequence (8 items):** daily story video lesson (Mon-Thu) -> illustrated
audio word cards -> picture-book reading & follow-along (Mon-Thu) -> multi-dimensional
quiz -> simulated foreign-teacher phone call ("Zebra Call") -> WeChat Chinese-teacher
mini-video (daily) -> WeChat group recorded-practice task & feedback (daily) -> Friday
interactive live-format class.

**Professional knowledge:** 3±2 short-term-memory-chunk cognitive-load ceiling for ages
3-7 motivating repetition design; 9+ weekly repetition-cycling target across varied
modalities; TPR/rhythm teaching methodology; multi-dimensional quiz-design principle;
build-vs-license (自研) content strategy.

**Evidence strength:** 8/8 items DIRECT-evidence-backed, independently re-verified across
4 total WebFetch passes (3 original + 1 during this recovery).

**IMPORTANT LIMITATION (this domain's central caveat, not shared by the other four):**
this source documents the **learner-facing weekly content-delivery/consumption
sequence** — what a child/parent experiences — **not** Zebra's internal content-
production (教研) pipeline. The source explicitly states it does not cover internal team
structure, production timeline, licensing/sourcing, or QA process. This is why
education_001 is rated **PARTIAL** rather than SUBSTANTIAL, while still being usable for
B3 as a content-structure/sequence reference (it maps directly onto Curify's own
education output-type baseline) rather than as a producer's step-by-step process
account. There is also a disclosed internal inconsistency in the source's own stated
weekly cadence vs. its day-specific component labels.

**Ready for B3:** YES, with the explicit caveat that this domain's "workflow" should be
read as content-type sequencing, not internal production steps, and its professional
constraints should be weighted more heavily than its step ordering.
