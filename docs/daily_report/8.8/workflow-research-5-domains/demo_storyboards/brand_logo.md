# Demo Storyboard — Brand/Logo

**Status:** Proposed Curify demo flow. Not a final video.

**Important baseline caveat:** unlike the other four domains, Curify's
*actual current* Brand/Logo workflow content is **not known** to this task.
The manager stated only that an external five-step brand-design process had
previously been reverse-engineered and adapted into a Curify demo — the five
steps themselves were never restated and are not reconstructed here (per
explicit instruction not to invent the missing baseline). There is no
`VIDEO_CONFIRMED` evidence for this domain at all.

Because of this, the storyboard below cannot be built the way the other four
were (start from Curify's own confirmed steps, add evidence-backed
refinements). Instead, it is built from general brand-identity domain logic
plus the external BRAND-03 case (`brand_logo_001`, `EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED`,
though its step **order** is itself a disclosed MEDIUM-confidence
reconstruction, not source-declared wording). **Almost every scene here is
tagged `PRODUCT_PROPOSAL` or `NEEDS_VALIDATION`, not `EVIDENCE_BACKED`** —
that is the correct, honest reflection of the evidence available, not an
oversight. Before this storyboard is used for real demo planning, the
manager's original five-step process should be recovered or re-documented so
a real current-vs-external comparison becomes possible.
