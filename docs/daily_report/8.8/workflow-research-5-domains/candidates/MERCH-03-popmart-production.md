## What was actually observed (EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED)

- **IP evaluation:** New IP assessed on trend-alignment, distinctive "character DNA" (example given: "Molly's pouting mouth and tear mole"), and extensibility across series. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **2D design phase:** "A new series from 2D design to final launch requires 8-12 months," compressed by pre-completing this phase during IP incubation. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **3D/technical phase:** Designs undergo "three-dimensional modeling, then engineering component separation and color application stages, determining product parts and painting details" before factory entry. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Review gate:** "The IP department convenes assessment meetings with product, sales, and consumer research teams to evaluate whether the IP and series products can achieve significant sales volume." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Production:** "Predictive production mode" — quantities estimated via multiple channels, orders adjusted on sales feedback; flagship IPs (e.g. Dimoo) kept "two to three complete series in reserve," compressing new-series cycles to "one to three months." Team includes "over 200 product designers" (2D/3D/packaging specialists). EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
