## What was actually observed (EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED)

- **Input:** On-site scouting/photography of NUS and NTU campus architecture (2018 and 2020 projects). EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Process — illustration:** "Large quantities of hand sketches to simplify architecture and visual effects"; one building reworked over five days due to composition dissatisfaction. Production workflow stated as: photograph → hand sketch tracing → line cleanup → AI (Adobe Illustrator) vector drawing → color blocking → Photoshop refinement → multi-size adaptation. Output rate cited as "one month yields ten illustrations, ~3 days per piece." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Process — product design (2 weeks):** Same illustration had to be re-composed per product format — postcard (horizontal), phone case (vertical), badge/magnet (square) — requiring "significant repositioning and supplementary painting" of backgrounds. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Process — packaging design (1 week):** Logo redesign, unboxing experience planning, branded kraft paper bags (minimum order 10,000 units), custom stamps, hand-folded boxes. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Process — sample testing/QC:** Multiple manufacturers tested for color accuracy, pricing, packaging spec compliance; defective units rejected (undersized sphere magnets, glass-damaged items, "ghosted" phone case prints), with losses absorbed by the designer. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Process — legal:** Consulted lawyers on Singapore's Trade Mark Act and Copyright Act; confirmed she could not use institutional logos/trademarked names and had to label products as unofficial merchandise. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Output:** Postcards, phone cases, refrigerator magnets, badges, mugs; Singapore-market pricing (SGD $1 postcards to $12 mugs) with bundle discounts. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
