## What was actually observed (EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED)

- **Input/problem:** Swoon (8 flavor variants) previously hired separate photographers per region (California, Austin, New York) for ecommerce vs. social shoots, creating inconsistent brand representation. Quote: "I would hire somebody for my ecomm photoshoots and then hire somebody for the social photoshoots." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Process:** Soona unified ecommerce + lifestyle photography into single shoots; stylists collaborated directly with Swoon's team to plan props/angles/setups per desired image, with live virtual-shoot feedback. Quote: "To be able to find a team that can do both lifestyle and product photography at the same time has been a huge blessing." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Output:** Amazon/retail listing imagery, lifestyle/movement shots (previously cost-prohibitive), hand-model photography, video, social ad assets, email/SMS marketing assets — all 8 flavors captured in one cohesive shoot. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Results:** First-month Green Tea launch sales reached 180 units vs. an 80-unit goal (125% over); product sold out within 3 months; content deployed across Amazon, Target, Kroger, Walmart, Whole Foods. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
