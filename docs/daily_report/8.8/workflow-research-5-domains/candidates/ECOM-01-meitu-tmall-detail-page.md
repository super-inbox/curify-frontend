## What was actually observed (EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED)

- **Input:** A single smartphone-captured photo of a collapsible water bottle, shot in an office with "clear lighting but cluttered background — desk reflections, pen holders, distant monitors." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Problem framing:** Article states many merchants skip detail-page optimization due to budget; reframes the fix as needing only "a clearly-shot product at proper angle" with sufficient lighting, not expensive equipment. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Commercial-insight translation:** Converting raw attributes into user benefits — for this bottle: "food-grade silicone fold structure, lightweight design, outdoor clip system" mapped to safety concerns, hiking-weight reduction, and portability pain points. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Style selection:** Four directional styles offered (outdoor vitality, tech aesthetic, home casual, luxury quality); testing showed "outdoor vitality style achieved notably higher click-through rates" for this sports-adjacent product. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Image-suite structure:** White-background shots (regulatory/multi-angle), scene images (kitchen counter, backpack side pocket), callout images (material/structural close-ups), comparison visuals (fold before/after). EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Tooling/efficiency claim:** Work "previously requiring a designer's two full days now compresses to under 30 minutes" using Meitu's AI product-photo tool. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Platform constraints:** Minimum 7 images recommended; white-background image mandatory for platform approval; 60%+ subject composition on primary images; vertical 3:4 ratio for mobile. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
