## What was actually observed (EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED)

- **Business context/brief:** Following the January 2020 merger of three insurance entities into Canada Life, the existing London Life "Constellation Managed Portfolios" tool/materials needed rebranding under the new corporate identity, with three goals: rebrand to Canada Life identity, increase advisor/client resonance via personas, and boost adoption by positioning Constellation as "simple, repeatable and scalable." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Research/audience strategy:** Three advisor personas (newer <4yr, tenured 10+yr, newer Asian-market advisors) each with 3-4 business goals; five client personas (small-business owners, young same-sex couples, middle-aged families, young adventurous couples, single high-net-worth individuals); secondary audience defined as mass-affluent clients ($25K+ minimum, ages 25-50). EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Localization constraint:** Materials produced in English, French, Mandarin, and Cantonese, with personas "modified for better alignment to the market segments" in the Mandarin/Cantonese versions. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **System device:** "A keyline square over photography" used exclusively on Constellation materials to distinguish it from other Canada Life collateral. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Applications:** Advisor brochure, client enrollment kits, die-cut brochures, large-format printed pieces, one-pagers/posters, digital-tool screenshots coordinated with an external vendor. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Launch/results:** Launched January 6, 2020; net sales rose to $123M (up $20M from 2019's $103M); 1,000+ advisors adopted the goals-based process; named client testimonial from Melanie Churchill (Associate Marketing Manager). EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.

## Verification caveat (added post-review)

The precise internal financial figures quoted above ("net sales rose to
$123M, up $20M from 2019's $103M," "1,000+ advisors adopted") come from an
individual employee's personal Behance portfolio post, not an official
Canada Life or agency publication. The figures are plausible and are
presented in the source as fact with a named client testimonial attached,
but they were not independently cross-verified against a second source.
Treat as EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED (i.e. genuinely stated on the cited page)
rather than independently corroborated.
