## What was actually observed (EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED)

- **Business context/input:** Palace Museum partnered with LKK to solve a stated consumer problem — existing gift merchandise was "numerous and chaotic" and failed to deliver storytelling/emotional value. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Research:** "On-site investigations of the Forbidden City and persona analysis of tourists," plus product-category organization and artifact/collection integration. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Process/scale:** "The entire group of 400 designers worked over 52 days through CBD crowdsourcing mode, researching and designing 17 themes with over 800 products." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Concept development:** Cats chosen for both practical (imperial pest control) and symbolic reasons (homophone with "longevity" in Chinese; frequent appearance in court paintings for auspicious meaning). The design goal was stated as giving the cat IP "storytelling, heritage, and innovation" while making derivative products "scenario-applicable, experiential, and adaptable." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Output:** "Approximately 200+ SKUs developed and produced," sold via the Palace Museum's official Tmall flagship store (the only IP-image product line there), a themed experience shop, and AI robots bearing the cat's likeness for visitor interaction. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
