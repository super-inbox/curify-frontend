## What was actually observed (EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED)

- **Brief:** Packaging a new pet product ("Combat Comb") for a startup entering an unfamiliar product category, targeting major-retailer distribution. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Structural design:** Product dimensions measured (width/length/depth); "a hanging box with a window" chosen to optimize shelf space while showing the product's function. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Mockup testing:** Physical prototypes iterated multiple times to test "sizing, stock weight, and the necessary folding" of internal structures, validating the angled platform and window placement. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Concept exploration:** Four design concepts developed against three goals (shelf impact, clear product communication, brand consistency); copy variations tested for audience resonance. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Dieline development:** Digital dieline built from mockup measurements, modified for internal structural elements; "3D software is used periodically to check the final dieline for accuracy." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Final production layout:** Approved design rendered onto the dieline with client feedback, product photography, feature callouts, and graphics emphasizing the comb's parting function. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Output:** Package approved, printed, and launched "in major retailers." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
