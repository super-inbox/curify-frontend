## What was actually observed (EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED)

- **Brief:** McDonald's (65M customers/day, 100+ countries) sought to evolve its 65-year-old brand while staying recognizable — "an extension of the transformation that kicked off a few years ago with our updated brand positioning" (Yehling). EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Strategy:** Pearlfisher centered the work on communicating joy; Campbell: McDonald's iconic status means "you can even just see a crop of it, and you understand what it is," and "packaging is a critical touchpoint for every single person who walks in the door." Team explored "how simple we could get this when we know we're going to have to work in multiple countries and languages." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Design system:** Custom typeface "Speedee Bold"; color palette derived from menu items; French-fry illustrations replacing prior yellow stripes; interactive arches on cups (e.g. transforming into flip-flops for summer); a deliberately imperfect, non-circular egg-yolk illustration on the Egg McMuffin wrapper, which Campbell called "the keystone to the whole system we built out of." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Constraint:** Cross-cultural and multilingual functional testing was required across the full system. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Explicit gap:** The article "does not specifically address materials, sustainability practices, or printing specifications" — confirmed absence, not fabricated.
