## What was actually observed (EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED)

- **Business context/brief:** Institute of Design (ID) at Illinois Tech, approaching its 85th anniversary, was perceived narrowly as "visual arts and product development" despite embodying advanced design thinking rooted in Bauhaus principles. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Research/strategy:** COLLINS conducted "historical reconnaissance" of ID's aesthetic/philosophical foundations, centering on László Moholy-Nagy's experimental geometry and Jay Doblin's systems thinking. Core positioning line: "Design is not what we make. Design is what we make possible." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Creative development:** "The Evolutionary" — a parametric design system where "six foundational structures" transform between abstract shapes, words, and images, mirroring design's movement "from chaos to clarity." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Identity system components:** Custom geometric "Mark of Lineage," modular stroke-based icon system (architecture, mobility, growth concepts), custom typeface, and a named set of "system augments" (Validated Versatility, Endurance Function, Flex Deck, Extensibility, Sticky Dissonance, Sandbox). EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Applications:** Deployed across admissions materials, signage, digital platforms, institutional communications; documented outcomes include improved brand consistency, internal alignment, and stronger recruitment narratives. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
