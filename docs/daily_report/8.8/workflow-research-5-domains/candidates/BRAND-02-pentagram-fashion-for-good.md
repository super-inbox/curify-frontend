## What was actually observed (EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED)

- **Business context/brief:** Fashion for Good (est. 2017, hubs in Amsterdam and Silicon Valley) needed "a brand strategy, visual identity and messaging platform" able to unite diverse stakeholders (brands, retailers, nonprofits, innovators, funders) around shared sustainability goals. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Research/strategy:** Grounded in William McDonough's Cradle to Cradle™ circular-fashion framework; articulated via "Five Goods" (Good Materials, Good Economy, Good Energy, Good Water, Good Lives) translating abstract sustainability into actionable categories. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Creative concept:** Core manifesto "Only good fashion," positioning sustainable fashion as "more attractive, accessible and affordable" than conventional alternatives. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Logo/identity development:** Typographic wordmark with "the overlapping 'good' running through 'fashion,'" rounded letterforms built from complete circles to embody "the circular and sustaining nature" of the philosophy. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **System:** Brand personality defined as "daring, style and practicality" with a "brave, wise and accessible" tone of voice; flexible colorway system lets partner organizations' primary colors run through the word "Good" in the brandmark. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Applications:** "Modern colour palettes, consistent layouts and inclusive imagery styles across print and digital applications," maintaining "a clear type-led aesthetic" while allowing campaign/partnership flexibility. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
