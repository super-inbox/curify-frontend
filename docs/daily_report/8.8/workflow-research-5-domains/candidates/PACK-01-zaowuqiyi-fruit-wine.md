## What was actually observed (EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED)

- **Selection stage (选品阶段):** Studio emphasizes trending-category selection tied to feasibility ("选品以热门领域，风口赛道为切入点"), including SWOT-style feasibility analysis. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Market research (市调阶段):** Consumer profiling, usage scenarios, competitive analysis; product framed around "基于'满足用户的情绪价值'而开发" (developed to satisfy users' emotional value). EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Product design (产品设计阶段):** Concept sketches, 3D modeling/rendering, and a stated visual-strategy checklist — "超级符号，卖点设计，元素构成，原创插画，字体设计，配色设计，板式设计" (super-symbol, selling-point design, element composition, original illustration, typography, color, layout). EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Finalization/production (定稿落地阶段):** White-sample (白样) structural/interaction testing, Pantone color-proof verification before mass production, technical-file/process-note delivery, die-cutting and gluing quality control. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Photography/launch (产品拍摄、宣发阶段):** Professional product photography and multi-platform marketing (Douyin, Xiaohongshu/Little Red Book, others). EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Studio philosophy quoted:** "包装即产品" (packaging as product itself) — advocating secondary functionality/collectibility beyond conventional bottle formats. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
