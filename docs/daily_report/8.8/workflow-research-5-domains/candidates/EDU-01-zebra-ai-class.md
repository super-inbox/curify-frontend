## What was actually observed (EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED)

- **Structure:** Weekly theme, "the first three days introducing new content and the final two days dedicated to review." Five daily video lessons "connect into a coherent story line" (fairy-tale drama format with real foreign teachers), matching children's concrete-thinking cognitive patterns. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Pedagogical/cognitive-science basis:** "3-7岁儿童的短时记忆容量仅为『3加减2』个组块" (3-7 year-olds' short-term memory holds only "3 plus/minus 2" chunks) — cited as the rationale for the repetition strategy. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Multi-dimensional vocabulary practice:** "一个单词，会分别出题练习『发音、含义、字形』" (a single word is tested across pronunciation, meaning, and character form). EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Lesson structure:** Video input (3-5 min story) → picture book + follow-along reading → quiz practice (varied dimensions) → "Zebra Call" simulated teacher phone interaction → Friday interactive AI classroom session. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Assets:** Story videos, illustrated word cards with audio, picture-book reading, follow-reading exercises, multiple-choice quizzes, WeChat-group homework submission with teacher feedback. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Output:** In-app task checklists and a parent-facing "学习报告" (learning report) providing progress visibility ("即时效果暗示" / immediate-effectiveness signal). EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.

Structurally near-identical to Curify's own education baseline (story/episode → word cards → reading & translation → quiz → character map → full learning pack) — this is the strongest external match found for the education domain, and it comes with real pedagogical/cognitive-science backing rather than being purely aesthetic.
