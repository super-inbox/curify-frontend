## What was actually observed (EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED)

- **Process stages (5-stage pipeline):**
  1. "Our talented team of writers and illustrators work together to create a first draft" — in-house educators with real teaching experience in the target country. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
  2. "Our editors review the content checking for mistakes as well as sensitivity or legal issues." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
  3. "Designers combine the illustrations and content to make a beautiful resource which will help children find the fun in learning." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
  4. "Reviews are conducted again to check for errors before the designs are published." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
  5. "The resource is checked regularly in case an update is required." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.
- **Stated quality/pedagogical principles:** Access, Accuracy and Integrity, Respect and Sensitivity, Inclusivity and Representation, Tailored Content, Ethical considerations in conflict-affected regions, and limited AI usage restricted from "core educational content." EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED.

## Limitation
