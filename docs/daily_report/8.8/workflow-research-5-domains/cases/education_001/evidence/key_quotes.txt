SECTION: 一、拆解还原：斑马英语的一周课程之旅？(Course Journey Breakdown)
================================================================================
- "Zebra English structures learning around weekly themes, with the first three days as new
   content days and the last two days as review days." (前三天为新课日，后两天为复习日)

1. Video (Story Drama Videos) — "Days 1-4 feature 3-5 minute fully English-language fairy
   tale dramas. Children infer plot while absorbing target vocabulary and sentence patterns."
   Episodes connect into one continuous storyline across the week.

2. TV Live Class (Interactive Video Drama) — "Friday's 20-minute session functions as a
   'weekly knowledge review.' It mimics 'foreign teacher live class with peer competition,'
   where children follow along with the teacher, using English to solve problems and advance
   the story."

3. Word (Vocabulary Cards) — "Illustrated electronic flashcards with audio."

4. Story & Speaking (Picture Book Reading & Follow-along) — "Days 1-4 include picture book
   reading followed by repetition practice, reinforcing daily lessons through new contextual
   stories."

5. Quiz (Assessment) — "采取的是最朴素的形式，没有做太多炫丽的包装。但在练习设计上，从多个维度
   练习同一个知识点" (uses the plainest format, without elaborate visual packaging; but in
   exercise design, the same knowledge point is practiced across multiple dimensions) —
   pronunciation, meaning, and written form.

6. Zebra Call (Foreign Teacher Simulated Phone) — "An external teacher appears on camera
   conversing with the child about surrounding objects, reviewing today's vocabulary through
   guided interaction with built-in response pauses."

7. WeChat Chinese Teacher Videos — "Daily video messages provide guided reading of the day's
   words and sentences, with pronunciation guidance."

8. WeChat Group Homework Service — "Daily tasks ask children to record videos using learned
   vocabulary/sentences in real contexts, submitting to the group. Teaching assistants provide
   individual voice feedback."

SECTION: 二、分析思考 > 1. 关键因素 (Analysis — Key Success Factors)
================================================================================
- Memory-capacity constraint: "3-7岁儿童的短时记忆容量仅为『3加减2』个组块，3岁基本只有无意识
   记忆，5岁之前普遍还没有形成记忆策略。" (3-7 year-old children's short-term memory capacity is
   only "3 plus/minus 2" chunks; at age 3 memory is essentially unconscious, and before age 5
   children generally have not yet formed memory strategies.)
- Repetition-cycling target: "这个单词在一周中至少被循环9+次，且形式多样，包含『视听输入，多通道
   呈现、多维度训练、书面语境中阅读、口头语境中交流、带读、开口跟读、自己表达』." (Each target word
   is cycled through at least 9+ times in a week, in varied formats: audio-visual input,
   multi-channel presentation, multi-dimensional practice, reading in written context, oral
   communication, guided reading, follow-along reading aloud, self-expression.)
- Teaching methodology: "外教综合运用韵律教学，tpr教学等方法，通过歌曲韵律+夸张的动作表情+不断
   重复." (Foreign teachers combine rhythm-based teaching and TPR methods, through
   song/rhythm + exaggerated expressions/gestures + continual repetition.)
- Learning reports referenced as an "immediate-effectiveness signal" (即时效果暗示) to parents,
  alongside seeing other children's practice videos in-group.

SECTION: 三、写在最后 (Closing Thoughts)
================================================================================
- "AI" branding described as still largely conceptual: "AI仍停留概念阶段" — despite the February
  2020 rename to "Zebra AI Class," only the final day of the 5-day trial has interactive
  content, and the pseudo-live follow-along assessment has known weaknesses (incorrect
  responses or desk-tapping can still register as correct/praised).
- Content density noted as relatively low compared to competitors: for the L2 level (ages
  6-8), only 3 daily words and 1-3 sentence patterns are taught, with two weekly days devoted
  solely to review.
- Self-developed content strategy: unlike most competitors who license existing textbooks and
  modify them, Zebra chose self-development (自研) — described as a harder, "different path,"
  offering flexibility and China-specific customization.
