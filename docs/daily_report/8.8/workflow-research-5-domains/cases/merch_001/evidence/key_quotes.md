## 【6】版权/法务 (Copyright/Legal)
- "特别小心谨慎的人" / "新加坡的法律特别特别严" — self-described as "exceptionally cautious" given "Singapore's particularly strict laws"
- "特别的查阅了商标权Trade Mark Act 和版权Copyright Act中所有相关章节" — "carefully reviewed all relevant chapters of the Trade Mark Act and Copyright Act"
- "咨询了律师再三确认我的产品不会侵犯版权" — "consulted lawyers multiple times to confirm products don't infringe"
- "不能使用学校的LOGO和学校注册过商标的名字" — "cannot use the school's LOGO or trademarked names"; "从外壳到芯子始终都没有出现NTU/NUS的 LOGO和学校全名" — "products never displayed NTU/NUS logos or full school names, from packaging to contents"
- "有些建筑是有建筑肖像权的，那些就不能画啦" — "some buildings have architectural portrait rights, which cannot be illustrated"

## 【7】定价 (Pricing)
- "自己是肯定不会定价便宜的啦，也不会走低价多销的方式" — "will not price cheaply; refuses low-price-high-volume strategy"
- "定价肯定是跟着新加坡的市场走的~一般一张明信片售价是$1, 一枚冰箱贴售价是$4, 学校卖的马克杯是一只$12" — "pricing follows the Singapore market: postcard ~$1 SGD, fridge magnet ~$4 SGD, mug ~$12 SGD"
- "成套的产品价格上给一个7-8折的优惠" — "7-8% [i.e., 20-30% off, "7-8折"] discount for complete sets" (折 = tenths of original price, so 7-8折 = 70-80% of price, a 20-30% discount)

- Phone case defects: "字儿有重影的或者切图不符合我效果图的部分" — "character/print shadowing or incorrect image cuts," requiring "退款处理" — "refund processing"
- "这部分占了我大部分的亏损。以后再进货我会再谨慎一点" — "this accounted for most of my losses; will be more careful with future orders"
