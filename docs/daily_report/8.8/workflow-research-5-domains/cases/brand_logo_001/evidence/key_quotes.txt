================================================================================
SECTION: Overview / Title
================================================================================
- "The rebrand and launch campaign of the Canada Life's Constellation managed portfolios
   program and digital tool"
- Author: Sabrina Young, RGD (Creative Design Lead / Sr. Graphic Designer)

================================================================================
SECTION: Objective
================================================================================
- (1) "Rebrand the existing London Life Constellation Managed Portfolios tool and materials
   to the new Canada Life brand"
- (2) "Help Constellation better resonate with advisors and their clients"
- (3) "Increase advisor awareness and adoption of the Constellation managed program"

================================================================================
SECTION: Target audiences
================================================================================
- Primary: newer advisors (under 4 years service) and tenured advisors (10+ years service)
- Secondary: "mass-affluent to affluent clients with a minimum of $25,000 to invest per
   household, targeting ages 25-50"
- Tertiary: newer advisors serving Asian-market/Chinese clients

================================================================================
SECTION: Client marketing materials
================================================================================
- Five client personas: small-business owners; young same-sex couple (starting a family);
  middle-aged family with children; young adventurous couple; single high-net-worth individual
- "The client kits were the primary client marketing materials produced."

================================================================================
SECTION: Advisor marketing materials
================================================================================
- Three advisor personas: newer advisors (under 4 years); tenured advisors (10+ years);
  newer advisors serving Asian-market clients
- "including an advisor brochure about how Constellation can help them find balance in their
   busy world"
- Keyline square device: "A keyline square over photography. The keyline draws attention to
   our client and advisor personas and only appears over photography on Constellation
   materials."
- Additional applications referenced across the client/advisor sections: die-cut brochures,
  large-format printed pieces, one-pagers/posters

================================================================================
SECTION: Challenges
================================================================================
Three named challenges:
1. Standing out / differentiating Constellation through design — positioning Constellation to
   excite advisors, emphasizing administrative relief so advisors could "focus on building
   client relationships and uncovering new opportunities."
2. Vendor coordination — "the digital Constellation tool is developed and updated by an
   external vendor. Coordinating the design and content updates with an external party
   presented another challenge." A "minimal viable product (MVP) approach" was used for
   initial launch materials.
3. Timeline pressure — regulatory requirements tied to the company amalgamation created "a
   firm launch deadline," requiring "significant overtime hours." (Confirmed explicitly as a
   third named challenge on the 2026-08-09 recovery re-fetch pass; treated as PARTIALLY
   corroborated context in evidence_manifest.csv since it was not equally explicit across all
   original fetch passes.)

================================================================================
SECTION: Mandarin and Cantonese adaptations
================================================================================
- Client and advisor personas were customized/"modified for better alignment" to Chinese-
  market segments
- Base languages English and French; "some printed materials were produced in Mandarin and
  Cantonese"

================================================================================
SECTION: Launch
================================================================================
- "Date: January 6, 2020"
- Languages: English, French, plus Mandarin/Cantonese for some printed materials

================================================================================
SECTION: Success and results (self-reported, PARTIAL support — not independently corroborated
against a second external source; excluded from formal workflow_steps and final_outputs)
================================================================================
- "Total net sales through Constellation for that year was $123 million. That's up $20 million
   from the $103 million total net sales in 2019"
- "helped more than 1,000 advisors take their clients through the goals-based investment
   process"
- Testimonial — Melanie Churchill, Associate Marketing Manager, Wealth Marketing, Canada Life:
  "The level of thought and understanding of the target markets...shone through in the
  creative concept." "...tailoring the client-facing materials to more broadly reflect
  different cultures and demographics in Canada, dramatically improved the reach."
