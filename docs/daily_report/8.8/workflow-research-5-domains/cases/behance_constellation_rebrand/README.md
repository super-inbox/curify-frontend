## Project name
CASE STUDY: Constellation Managed Portfolios — "Constellation rebrand and launch campaign"

## Platform
Behance (project owner: Sabrina Young, RGD; published December 12, 2023)

## Case overview
The case documents the 2020 rebrand and relaunch of Canada Life's "Constellation" managed
portfolios program and its companion digital advisor tool, triggered by the merger of London
Life, Great-West Life, and Canada Life into the new Canada Life. The work spanned advisor-facing
and client-facing marketing materials in English and French, with select pieces localized into
Mandarin and Cantonese for Chinese-Canadian advisor and client segments. Deliverables included
client enrollment kits, an advisor brochure, and updates to a vendor-built digital tool. Reported
outcome: total net sales through Constellation rose from $103M (2019, under the old London Life
brand) to $123M (2020, post-relaunch), with adoption by 1,000+ advisors.

## Observed creative workflow steps
1. **Brand consolidation** — map the legacy Constellation/London Life visual identity onto the
   newly merged Canada Life brand system (look, feel, and messaging).
2. **Audience segmentation & persona development** — define 3 advisor personas (newer advisors
   <4 yrs, newer Chinese advisors, tenured advisors 10+ yrs) and 5 client personas (small business
   owner, young same-sex couple, middle-aged family, adventurous couple, high-net-worth individual),
   each paired with distinct life/financial goals and matching photography.
3. **Differentiation device** — introduce a recurring design element (a keyline square overlaid on
   persona photography) used only on Constellation materials to make them identifiable across the
   broader Canada Life material set.
4. **Localization pass** — adapt all 5 client personas and 2 of 3 advisor personas into Mandarin/
   Cantonese versions for the Chinese-market segment, alongside the English/French base materials.
5. **Cross-vendor coordination** — since the digital tool was built/maintained by an external
   vendor, the team used an MVP approach: ship materials with minimal placeholder screenshots first,
   then swap in fuller screenshots from the vendor after launch.
6. **Production under a fixed regulatory deadline** — the launch date was fixed by the Canada Life
   Assurance Company amalgamation's regulatory requirements, so the team compressed the schedule
   with overtime rather than moving the date.
7. **Collateral production** — advisor brochure, client kits (used by advisors during client
   enrollment conversations), and supporting print/large-format materials.
8. **Post-launch measurement** — track adoption (advisor count) and net sales year-over-year as the
   success metric, with a client testimonial from the marketing stakeholder included as validation.

## Why this case is useful for workflow research
- It is a documented, real (non-hypothetical) B2B2C financial-services rebrand with named
  before/after business metrics, not just a visual portfolio piece — useful as a workflow reference
  point where creative decisions are tied to measurable outcomes.
- The persona-driven design system (3 advisor + 5 client personas, each with a repeatable
  photography/goal pairing) is a concrete, reusable pattern for how a "personas → creative system"
  pipeline can be structured across a large multi-language material set.
- It explicitly narrates constraints that shape real creative workflows but are often invisible in
  polished case studies: a hard regulatory deadline, dependency on an external vendor for a digital
  surface, and a phased/MVP delivery approach to manage that dependency.
- It includes a full credits breakdown (creative lead, supporting designers, communication
  specialists, production coordinators), which is useful for understanding team composition and
  role division on a project of this scope.
