## Input

A single smartphone-captured photo of a collapsible/foldable outdoor water bottle, taken at an office desk. Adequate lighting but cluttered background (desk reflections, a pen holder, a monitor in the distance) and no "outdoor" visual context. The source explicitly frames this as proof that professional studio photography is not required — "a phone-shot image with clear product and correct angle is completely sufficient."

## Verified Workflow Sequence (EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFIRMED only)

All five steps below are named and ordered in the source under the section heading "从'毛坯'到'精装'：一张原图的逆袭":

1. **原片入场 (Raw photo intake)** — Upload the raw office-desk phone photo to the tool's backend.
2. **核心指令 (Core instructions / selling-point input)** — Input specific product selling points as text: "食品级硅胶折叠结构、轻量化设计、户外挂扣系统" (food-grade silicone folding structure, lightweight design, outdoor carabiner/clip system). Source states each phrase is chosen deliberately to map to a consumer concern (food-grade → safety; lightweight → hiking pain point; carabiner system → functional differentiation).
3. **风格定调 (Style selection)** — Choose one of four defined visual-direction styles (outdoor vitality / tech quality / home casual / luxury). Source states the outdoor-vitality style was selected because testing showed it produced higher click-through rates for this product category.
4. **套图生成 (Image-suite generation)** — Choose "smart matching" or "custom configuration" to generate a minimum-7-image suite spanning four required image categories: white-background shots (folded + unfolded), scene images (kitchen counter, backpack pocket), selling-point close-ups (material, carabiner detail), and a system-recommended fold/unfold comparison image.
5. **生成与落位 (Generation and page placement)** — Render the final high-quality assets and place them into the existing detail-page layout/framework.

## Outputs

A stylistically unified set of at least 7 images meeting Tmall platform composition norms (white-background mandatory shot, ≥60% product-body composition, 3:4 vertical ratio for mobile), assembled into a finished product detail page. Source states the integrated page shows increased "trust factor and immersion" even without additional layout/typography work.

## Professional Knowledge / Industry Constraints Found (source-confirmed)

- Minimum image count of 7 for adequate "information density" on a detail page.
- Four required image-type categories (white-background / scene / selling-point close-up / comparison).
- White-background image is described as a hard requirement.
- Recommended ≥60% product-body proportion within the frame, to avoid backgrounds crowding out the subject.
- 3:4 vertical image ratio recommended specifically to maximize mobile-screen utilization.
- Efficiency claim: work "previously requiring a designer two days" compressed to "under thirty minutes" using the AI tool, with quality described as "more stable and controllable."
